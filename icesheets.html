Ice Sheet Thickness & Elevation Histograms

Visualize the distribution of ice thickness, surface elevation, and bedrock depth for Greenland and Antarctica using data from NASA's IceBridge program and NSIDC archives.

Data Visualization Overview

This dashboard provides detailed statistical distributions of ice sheet properties. The interactive charts display:

  • Ice Thickness: Distribution of ice volume across the sheets.
  • Surface Elevation: Variations in surface height relative to sea level.
  • Bedrock Elevation: Topography beneath the ice, critical for understanding bed conditions and flow dynamics.
