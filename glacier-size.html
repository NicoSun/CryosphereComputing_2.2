Glacier Frontal Area & Thickness Profiles

Visual comparison of glacier frontal area and thickness profiles using line charts. These visualizations depict ice height (meters) versus distance along the glacier front (kilometers), allowing for direct comparison of major Antarctic and Greenland glaciers.

Data Interpretation

The line charts below represent cross-sections along the glacier fronts.

  • Y-Axis: Height in meters (relative to sea level).
  • X-Axis: Distance along the width of the glacier (varies by chart)
  • Note on Thickness: The vertical difference between surface and bedrock represents thickness only for grounded ice. Floating ice shelves will show different characteristics based on draft depth.
